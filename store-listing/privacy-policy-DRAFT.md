## In short

JLPT Quest is designed to work offline. Your learning progress — XP, streaks, stars,
lesson results, review cards and weak spots — is stored on your own device. We do not
run an account system, we do not collect your learning data on a server, and we do not
show ads inside lessons. The one exception is purchases: if you buy Premium, that
purchase is processed by Google Play, under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

## Data we collect

**We do not collect, store, or receive your personal data on any server.**

In particular, version 0.1 of JLPT Quest:

- Has no user accounts and no sign-in.
- Has no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK that sends data off your device.
- Does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or device identifiers.
- Does not sell or share your data with anyone, because we do not receive it.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following **only on your device**, so it can work offline and
remember where you left off:

- Your placement result, daily goal, and learning preferences.
- Lesson progress, XP, levels, streaks, stars, and achievements.
- Review scheduling data (FSRS cards) and the weak-spot list built from your mistakes.
- Whether you hold an active Premium entitlement, as reported by Google Play.

This data never leaves your device through the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing
its storage in your device settings, deletes it.

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Premium is sold through Google Play Billing:

- Monthly subscription: `premium_monthly` — $7.99/month (US price; local prices vary).
- Yearly subscription: `premium_yearly` — $47.99/year, with a 7-day free trial where offered.
- Lifetime purchase: `premium_lifetime` — $179 one-time.

Payment details are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card number or
payment credentials. Google may share with us only what is needed to confirm a purchase
— such as the product purchased and its status — under Google's policies. Manage,
cancel, or request a refund for a subscription in Google Play, not in the app:
Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

## Listening (text-to-speech)

Listening exercises use your device's own Japanese text-to-speech engine. The app does
not send your lesson content to a speech server. Your device manufacturer's own
text-to-speech service may have its own terms; this policy covers JLPT Quest only.

## Children's privacy

JLPT Quest is intended for users aged 13 and over (general audience). It is not
designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
Because version 0.1 has no accounts and no server data collection, there is no
account data to request or delete for any user.

## Deleting your data

There is no account to delete in version 0.1. To erase all of your JLPT Quest data,
uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings. If you have a
purchase question, contact us at the support email above, or manage the purchase
in Google Play as described under "Purchases".

## Third parties

The only third party involved in version 0.1 is Google Play, for app distribution
and purchases. No other third-party SDK, analytics provider, advertiser, or server
receives data from the app.

## If the app changes

**Re-check this policy before production launch if any of these are added:**
