## Full description (draft)
**One quest from your first word to JLPT day.**

JLPT Quest is a game-first Japanese course built for one job: helping you pass the JLPT. No wandering through random phrases — every lesson sits on the exam path for your level.

**A path, not a pile of flashcards**
Travel a quest map through themed chapters. Each chapter teaches vocabulary, kanji and grammar, then tests you with listening, a short reading — and a timed boss fight that works like a mini JLPT.

**Reviews that respect your time**
JLPT Quest uses FSRS spaced repetition (the modern successor to SM-2) and schedules each word, kanji and grammar point just before you would forget it — the same retention with fewer reviews. Miss something? It goes to your weak-spot deck, not a punishment screen. There are no hearts and nothing blocks you from learning.

**Mocks that tell the truth**
Timed mock exams scored out of 180 with section bars, exactly like the real test's structure. You'll see the trap that catches real candidates: passing the total while failing a section minimum — and precisely which skill to fix next. Scores are honest estimates, never guarantees.

**Built for real life**
• 5-minute lessons designed for commutes and lunch breaks
• Works fully offline — learn on the train
• Listening with adjustable speed and a text fallback for quiet places
• Dark mode, large text, TalkBack support, furigana controls on the way

**Free and Premium**
The full N5 path is free, forever. Premium (one simple subscription) removes the daily new-item cap, unlocks every mock exam with per-item explanations, offline packs for every level, and advanced weak-spot training. N4 arrives next, then N3, N2 and N1 — one engine, five worlds, and your progress carries over.

JLPT Quest contains only original practice material written for this app. It is not affiliated with the Japan Foundation or JEES, and it never uses real JLPT questions.

Questions or a mistake to report? Every exercise has a report button, and we read every one.
